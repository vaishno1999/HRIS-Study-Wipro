# HRIS-Study-Wipro
# A Study on Human Resource Information System in Wipro Pvt. Ltd., Nagpur

## 📌 Project Overview

This MBA research project focuses on studying the **Human Resource Information System (HRIS)** and its role in managing employee-related information and supporting HR activities.

The study examines the use, importance, challenges, and requirements of HRIS, with a focus on employee perspectives in Wipro Pvt. Ltd., Nagpur.

## 🎓 Project Details

* **Project Type:** MBA Academic Research Project
* **Organization Studied:** Wipro Pvt. Ltd., Nagpur
* **Research Area:** Human Resource Management
* **Topic:** Human Resource Information System (HRIS)

## 🎯 Objectives

* To study the Human Resource Information System.
* To understand the importance of HRIS in HR activities.
* To identify problems associated with HRIS.
* To study the requirements for effective HRIS implementation.
* To examine the relationship between HRIS and training-related activities.

## 🔬 Research Methodology

* **Research Design:** Descriptive Research
* **Sampling Technique:** Random Sampling
* **Primary Data:** Questionnaire and personal interviews
* **Secondary Data:** Books, websites, reports, and research material
* **Analysis Techniques:** Percentage analysis, tables, charts, and graphical representation

## 📊 Research Scope

The study focuses on employees/users of HRIS in Nagpur and examines their perspectives regarding HRIS usage and its role in HR management.
